Overview

Describe how enzyme-linked receptors work.

  • At least two ligands bind to the receptor which causes clustering of receptors.
  • The clustering activates the enzymes that are linked to the receptors.
  • The active enzymes then phosphorylate the receptor.
  • Phosphorylation leads to the binding of signalling proteins to the cytoplasmic domain of the receptor.
  • These signalling proteins recruit other signalling proteins and signal is generated throughout the cell.

Lecture notes 2.10 Cell Signalling.

Recall two examples of enzyme-linked receptors.

  • Receptor tyrosine kinases (RTKs) — e.g. insulin receptor, EGFR / growth-factor receptors.
  • Receptor serine/threonine kinases — e.g. TGF-β receptors.
  • Cytokine receptors that signal via associated JAKs are also taught as enzyme-linked receptor systems.
